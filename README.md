## display pins (2.8 TFT SPI 240x320):
> VCC - 3v3
> GND - GND  
> CS - 15  
> RESET - 4  
> DC - 2  
> SD1(MOSI) - 23  
> SCK - 18  
> LED - 3v3  
> SD0(MISO) - 19  
> T_CLK - 18  
> T_CS - 21  
> T_DIN - 23  
> T_D0 - 19  
> T_IRQ - (пусто, do not connect)  

##  
  > [!WARNING]
> do not apply more than 5 volts to the oscilloscope pin!
